Struck-corner F — variations

Eleven directions on the struck-corner theme. Partial cuts feel blockier and rounder; edge-to-edge cuts feel sharper and more aggressive. All built on the same 8px grid.

A. Original struck your V1

Single 16px chamfer on the top-right of the top arm. Leaves a flat top edge — reads as a normal F until you look twice.
